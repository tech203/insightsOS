Common Questions

Clear answers so pricing feels easier to understand.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are month-to-month with no contract. Cancel from the Stripe portal and you keep access until the end of the current billing period.

What happens if I run out of credits mid-month?

Your workspace stays open. Credit-gated actions (briefs, drafts, audits) pause until you top up or the next billing cycle resets your included credits.

Do I have to talk to sales?

No. Free, Pro, and Growth are fully self-serve through Stripe. Reach out only if you want a custom Agency or white-label arrangement.

Do plans and credits mean the same thing?

No. Your plan controls workspace and feature access. Credits are the meter for AI actions like briefs, drafts, and audits — included with every plan and toppable any time.

Start small, then scale when needed

Begin with a simple plan, add credits when usage grows, and only unlock agency delivery features when your workflow requires them.
