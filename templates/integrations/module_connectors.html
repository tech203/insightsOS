Module connectors

Foundation connectors for the Ecommerce and Website modules. These connect your platform credentials so future audit + publish flows can pick them up. Not yet wired into the audit pipeline or the content publish path.

These five connectors are foundation only — connecting verifies your credentials and stores them, but no catalog sync, audit, or publish happens yet. Each will get full module wiring in follow-up releases.
