{# =========================================================== ACCOUNT & SIGN-IN =========================================================== #}

Account & sign-in

Create an account, verify your email, reset a password, or sign in with Google.

Create an account

  1. Go to Sign up from the marketing site or invitation email.
  2. Enter your name, email, and a password (or click Continue with Google).
  3. Open the verification email and click the link to activate your account.
  4. You will land on the dashboard ready to add your first workspace.
{{ shot('signup-form', 'Signup form — name, email, password, or one-click Google.') }}

Verify your email

If you did not receive the email, open Settings → Account and click Resend verification. The link expires after 24 hours; you can request a new one any time.

Forgot password

  1. From the login page click Forgot password?
  2. Enter your email; a reset link arrives within a minute.
  3. Open the link, choose a new password, and sign in.
{{ shot('forgot-password', 'Forgot password — enter your email and a reset link is sent.') }}

Sign in with Google

Click Continue with Google on the login page. If your Google email matches an existing account they will be linked automatically.

{{ shot('login-form', 'Login screen with email/password and Continue with Google.') }}
{# =========================================================== WORKSPACES =========================================================== #}

Workspaces

Each brand or business lives in its own workspace. Audits, content, and integrations are scoped to it.

Create a workspace

  1. Open Workspaces from the sidebar.
  2. Click + Add Workspace.
  3. Fill in business name, website URL, industry, and location.
  4. Save. The workspace appears in the list and opens to its overview.
{{ shot('workspace-new-form', 'New workspace form — only name and website are required.') }}

Workspaces list

The Workspaces page shows every brand you track at a glance. Each tile links to that workspace’s overview.

{{ shot('workspaces-list', 'Workspaces list — one tile per brand.') }}

Workspace overview

The overview ties everything together: score, next best step, recommended actions, integrations, recent audits.

{{ shot('workspace-overview', 'Workspace overview — score, priorities, integrations, and content opportunities in one place.') }}

Edit, archive, or delete a workspace

  • Edit: Workspace overview → Edit button (top right).
  • Delete: Edit page → Delete workspace. This is permanent.
  • Reactivate: If you released a workspace seat, you can reactivate it from Settings → Workspaces.

Upload a logo

From the workspace overview click the logo placeholder and upload a square image. You can remove it any time.

{# =========================================================== AUDITS =========================================================== #}

Audits

An audit checks how visible your brand is in AI answers and modern search.

Run an audit

  1. Open the workspace → click Run audit (or use Start audit from the dashboard).
  2. Confirm credits to spend. Each audit deducts from your wallet.
  3. Wait for completion — usually under two minutes. You will see a progress card.
  4. The workspace overview refreshes with a new score, missed mentions, and recommended actions.

View past audits

Workspace → History lists every audit with date, score, and a link to the full report.

{{ shot('workspace-history', 'Workspace history — every previous audit run.') }}

Export an audit as PDF

Open the report → click Export PDF at the top right.

{# =========================================================== CONTENT BRIEFS & DRAFTS =========================================================== #}

Content briefs & drafts

Turn audit recommendations into briefs your team can review or drafts ready to publish.

Create a brief

  1. Workspace → Content brief.
  2. Enter or pick a query. Optional: choose tone and target page type.
  3. Click Generate brief. The brief includes structure, key sections, and target keywords.
  4. Save brief to add it to the content queue.
{{ shot('brief-form', 'Content brief form — pick a topic and tone.') }}

Create a draft

  1. Workspace → Content draft (or click Generate draft from a brief).
  2. Confirm the topic and tone, then click Generate.
  3. Review the draft inline; Save draft sends it to the content queue.
{{ shot('draft-form', 'Content draft form — same flow as briefs, end-to-end output.') }}

Query ideas

If you’re not sure what to write about, open Query ideas from the workspace for a fresh list of question-shaped topics tied to your industry.

{{ shot('query-ideas', 'Query ideas — ready-made topic prompts based on the workspace.') }}

Brief vs. draft — which first?

Use a brief when you want structure and stakeholder approval before writing. Use a draft when the topic is clear and you want speed.

{# =========================================================== CONTENT QUEUE =========================================================== #}

Content queue

Every brief and draft flows through the queue until it is approved and published.

Statuses

  • Idea / Brief / Draft — work-in-progress stages.
  • Approved — locked for publishing.
  • Published — sent to an integration or marked done.
{{ shot('content-queue', 'Content queue — items moving from idea to published.') }}

AI edit a draft

  1. Open the draft in the queue.
  2. Click AI edit; pick a section or the whole document.
  3. Describe the change (“shorter intro”, “add 3 FAQs”) and apply.
  4. Edit history lets you revert any change.

Generate a visual

From an open draft click Generate visual. The platform uses your brand kit to produce a header image you can attach to the post.

Approve and publish

  1. Click Approve on the queue item.
  2. Pick a destination: Webflow, Wix, Framer, Squarespace, Shopify, or mark as published manually.
  3. Publishing pushes the post to the CMS; Update on site re-syncs changes after edits.

{# =========================================================== REPORTS & SHARING =========================================================== #}

Reports & sharing

Send a clean view of audit findings to clients or stakeholders.

  1. Workspace → Report.
  2. Click Share → toggle on Public link.
  3. Copy the URL. Anyone with the link sees a read-only version.
  4. Toggle off any time to revoke access.

Use Export PDF on the report page to download a printable copy. Presentation mode is available from the workspace overview.

{# =========================================================== INTEGRATIONS =========================================================== #}

Integrations

Connect a workspace to your CMS, analytics, or booking tools.

Where to manage integrations

Open the workspace overview — the Integrations section lists every tile with status, last sync time, and a connect/disconnect button. For low-level credentials see Module connectors.

{{ shot('integrations-grid', 'Module connectors — store credentials per integration.') }}

Webflow

  1. Integrations → Webflow → Settings.
  2. Paste your Webflow API token; click Test connection.
  3. Pick the target site and collections (Blog, FAQ, Service).
  4. From the content queue use Publish → Webflow on any approved item.

Wix, Framer, Squarespace

Each works the same: open the tile → click Connect → authorise the account → optionally Refresh collections. Then publish from the queue.

Shopify

  1. Integrations → Shopify → Connect.
  2. Approve access in the Shopify admin pop-up.
  3. Click Sync products; the platform pulls product titles and descriptions.
  4. Use Preview alt text fixes or Preview description rewrites, then Apply to push changes.

BigCommerce, Shopline, WooCommerce

Connect by pasting store credentials, then run Sync to pull catalogue data.

Google Search Console

  1. Integrations → Google Search Console → Connect and sign in with Google.
  2. Pick the verified property that matches the workspace URL.
  3. Click Sync. Recent impressions and clicks appear inside the workspace.

Google Analytics

Connect with Google, choose a GA4 property, then sync. Disconnect any time from the tile.

Cal.com

Paste your Cal.com API key in the tile, click Connect, then Sync to pull bookings and event types.

{# =========================================================== FAQ =========================================================== #}

Frequently asked questions

The questions people send us most.

Do I need to understand the score deeply?

No. The score is a shortcut: strong, moderate, or early-stage. The priorities tell you what to do next.

What should I open right after an audit?

The workspace overview. The Next Best Step card surfaces the top action and the priority list shows the rest in order.

Should I create a brief or a draft first?

Use a brief for structure and approval first. Use a draft when the topic is clear and you want speed.

When should I re-run an audit?

After important new pages are published, after major revisions, or when you want to check whether visibility is improving.

How many credits does an audit cost?

Each audit deducts from your wallet at run time. The confirmation modal shows the exact cost before you confirm.

What happens if I run out of credits mid-audit?

Credits are reserved before an audit starts, so this cannot happen. If reservation fails the audit will not begin and you will be prompted to top up.

Do credits expire?

Bundle credits never expire. Monthly plan credits reset at the start of each billing cycle.

What is the difference between a workspace and a team seat?

A workspace is a brand or business you track. A team seat is a person who can sign into your account.

Can I move a workspace to another account?

Not yet — contact support if you need this.

What happens when I release a workspace?

It is archived and the seat returns to your pool. Reactivate any time from Settings → Workspaces.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Settings → Billing → Open Stripe portal → Cancel. Access continues until the end of the current period.

Can I get a refund on an unused bundle?

Unused credit bundles are refundable within 14 days of purchase. Reach out to support with your invoice number.

How do I connect Webflow?

Open the workspace → Integrations → Webflow → paste your API token → pick collections. See the Integrations section above for details.

My Google Search Console connect button does nothing.

Make sure pop-ups are allowed for this domain. If the issue continues, sign out of every Google account except the one with verified properties.

Why did my draft fail to generate?

The most common cause is missing brand context. Open the workspace → Brand context → fill the required fields, then try again.

Can I edit a generated draft after publishing?

Yes. Edit in the content queue, then use Update on site on the queue item to re-sync to the CMS.

How do public report links work?

They are read-only, no login required, and can be revoked instantly by toggling the share switch off.

Does white-label apply to PDFs too?

Yes. Once a logo and colour are saved, all shared reports and PDF exports use them.

Can clients log in to see their report?

Use the public share link for read-only access. For collaboration, invite them as a team seat.

Why is the Growth Calendar empty?

Recommendations come from your latest audit. Run an audit first; the calendar populates within a minute of completion.

How do I add a competitor manually?

Workspace → Competitors → Add competitor. Enter name and website. They are included in the next audit.

My logo upload was rejected.

Use a square PNG or JPG under 2 MB. Transparent backgrounds work best for dark-mode users.

Can I run audits on a single page rather than a whole site?

Audits are workspace-level, but you can use Answer Monitor to track specific queries that map to a single page.

Where do I see invoices?

Stripe portal (Settings → Billing → Open Stripe portal) keeps every invoice and receipt.

{# =========================================================== TROUBLESHOOTING =========================================================== #}

Troubleshooting

What to try when something is not working.

I clicked Run audit but nothing happened

Check your credit balance in the top bar. If it shows 0 the run was blocked before starting — top up and try again.

Integration shows “disconnected” after working before

OAuth tokens expire. Open the tile → Reconnect. Your previously synced data is preserved.

Publishing to Webflow / Wix failed

Open the queue item → expand the error. Most failures are missing required fields in the target collection. Map them in Integration settings.

Cannot sign in with Google

If the account already exists with a password, sign in that way once — Google linking happens automatically on success.

Email verification link expired

Settings → Account → Resend verification. Links last 24 hours.

Glossary

Plain meanings, no jargon.

AEO

Helping your business show up in AI answers and modern search journeys.

Visibility

Whether your business appears when people ask AI tools or search.

Audit

A report that checks visibility, gaps, and improvement opportunities.

Growth Calendar

A 4-week schedule built from audit recommendations.

Content Brief

A structured outline for a page or article your team will create.

Content Draft

A first working version of content generated from your brief.

Competitor Gap

A query where competitors appear but your business does not.

Schema

Machine-readable labels that help search systems understand your business.

Credit

A unit spent to run audits and generation jobs. Stored in your wallet.

Workspace

One brand or business you track. Audits and content sit inside it.

Team seat

A person who can sign into your account. Different from a workspace.

Brand kit

Colours, fonts, and visual rules reused for generated pages and visuals.
