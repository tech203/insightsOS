How it works

Three rules so it stays simple.

  1. Free signups don't pay out — the link only earns when the referred user actually pays.
  2. You earn 25% of the dollar value they pay (subscription or credit bundle), as credits in your wallet.
  3. The 30-day window starts at signup. Every paid purchase inside that window earns — outside it, the referral expires.

Example: a referred user signs up free, then upgrades to Pro ($49/mo) on day 8 and buys a 20-credit bundle ($19) on day 22. You earn 25% × ($49 + $19) = 17 credits.
