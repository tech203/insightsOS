This Privacy Policy describes what data DarInsights ("we", "us") collects when you use our service, how we use it, who we share it with, and how you can ask us to delete it. We try to keep this short and honest.

What we collect

Account data you provide

  • Email + display name — used as your login identifier and to address you in product communication.
  • Password (hashed) — we never store your plaintext password. Hashes use scrypt with a per-user salt.
  • Workspace fields you enter (website URL, industry, location, Brand Kit details, business profile).

Public website content we crawl

To run an audit we fetch your publicly-available web pages — the same content any search engine reads. We never receive login credentials and never crawl private pages. Audited content stays associated with your account only.

Payment information

Card details are entered directly into Stripe's hosted checkout and never touch our servers. We store only the Stripe customer ID + subscription ID needed to look up your plan and process cancellations.

Operational data

  • IP address + user-agent for rate limiting and security event logging.
  • Request timestamps and route paths for performance monitoring.
  • Email-delivery records (sent / bounced / unsubscribed).

How we use it

  • Operate the product — generate audits, render dashboards, send transactional email (verification, password reset, team invites).
  • Bill paid plans — pass invoice events to Stripe for processing.
  • Protect against abuse — rate limiting, security event review, fraud detection.
  • Improve the product — aggregate usage patterns (never identifying individual workspaces) inform what we build next.

We do not sell your data, do not train AI models on your audit results, and do not use your workspace content to improve someone else's audits.

Who we share it with

A small number of sub-processors execute parts of the service on our behalf. Each operates under a Data Processing Agreement that binds them to the same protections this policy describes.

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • OpenAI — language-model inference for audit scoring + content generation. Audit content is sent as untrusted data with prompt-injection fencing; OpenAI's API terms prohibit training on customer data.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery.
  • AWS / cloud host — application hosting + file storage.
  • Google APIs (Search Console, Analytics) — only when you connect those integrations.

Your rights

You can, at any time, from Settings → Account:

  • Export your data — download a JSON bundle of everything we hold about you (profile, wallet, workspaces, audit summaries).
  • Delete your account — irreversibly remove your account + all owned data, with best-effort Stripe subscription cancellation. The deletion cascades across ~20 tables; see the source for the exact list.
  • Update your display name.
  • Change your password — all existing sessions are invalidated immediately, including ones on other devices.

For requests we can't surface in-app (data correction, sub-processor inquiries, GDPR/CCPA subject access), email hello@darinsights.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Retention

  • Active accounts — data is kept for the life of the account.
  • Deleted accounts — purged within 7 days. Backups containing residual data are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Email-delivery logs — kept for 90 days for deliverability debugging, then purged.
  • Stripe records — Stripe retains payment records per its own retention policy (required for tax + dispute reasons); we don't control this.

Security

  • HTTPS-only with HSTS for all traffic to our domain.
  • Passwords hashed with scrypt + per-user salts.
  • Sessions invalidated automatically on password change.
  • Rate limiting on all auth + email-sending endpoints.
  • Cross-tenant isolation enforced at every workspace boundary; regression-tested.
  • Security-incident contact: security@darinsights.com.

Cookies

We use a single first-party session cookie to keep you logged in. It's HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and Secure in production. We do not use third-party tracking cookies on the marketing pages.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be announced via in-app notice + email at least 14 days before they take effect. The "Effective" date at the top of the page is updated on every revision; previous versions are available on request.

Contact

DarInsights — operated by Astra Collective Asia. Questions: hello@darinsights.com. Privacy / data requests: privacy@darinsights.com. Security incidents: security@darinsights.com.
