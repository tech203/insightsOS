FAQ

Quick answers

What does DarInsights do?

DarInsights audits how often your brand appears in AI-generated answers — across Google AI, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini — identifies the queries you're missing, surfaces which competitors are winning the citations instead, and turns those findings into content briefs and drafts you can ship.

Can I sign up right now?

Yes — sign up is open and free. Type your website into the form above, complete the 30-second signup, and your first audit runs immediately. The Free plan includes 3 starter credits — enough to run your first audit with no card on file.

What happens to my website data?

We fetch only your publicly-available web pages (the same content any search engine reads) to run the audit. We never receive login credentials and never crawl private pages. Audit results are readable only by you and people you explicitly invite to your team. Full erasure available anytime from Settings → Account.

How accurate are the AI engine results?

We query each engine the same way a real buyer would, then count brand mentions and ranking position in the assistant's answer. Results reflect what those engines actually returned at audit time — they can shift as the engines retrain, which is why we let you re-audit anytime to track movement (instead of treating one snapshot as truth).

Do I need to install anything on my site?

No. No scripts, no DNS changes, no analytics snippets. We work entirely from the outside — the same vantage point an AI engine has. Optional integrations (Google Search Console, Google Analytics, Shopify, Webflow) layer on extra signal but are not required to run an audit.

How is this different from SEO tools like Ahrefs or Semrush?

SEO tools measure how you rank in blue-link search results. DarInsights measures whether AI assistants cite your brand by name in their answers — a different surface that's increasingly where buyers form their shortlist. Both matter; we focus on the AI side because nobody else does.

What if my brand doesn't appear in any of the results?

That's the most common starting point — and the most useful audit. You'll get a list of the specific queries you're missing, the competitor brands appearing instead, and a prioritised set of content actions to start closing the gap. The Pro plan generates briefs + drafts in the same workflow, so you can ship the first piece the same day.

Do I need to pay now?

No card on signup. Your first audit runs on the 3 starter credits the Free plan includes. Upgrade only when you want more audits, more workspaces, or the content-brief / draft generation in the Pro and Growth plans.

Who is it for?

Agencies running AI visibility for a roster of clients, in-house teams who own their own brand's visibility, B2B companies whose buyers research in AI assistants before booking, and local service businesses showing up in "best X near me" AI answers.

Can I request a walkthrough?

Yes — especially useful if you're an agency considering it for client reporting, or running an early pilot. Book a 30-min strategy call and we'll walk through your specific use case.
